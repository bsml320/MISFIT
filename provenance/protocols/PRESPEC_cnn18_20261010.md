# Prespecification: 18-fit OASIS-3 CNN study (frozen 2026-10-10, before execution)

PI-directed study. Two deliverables: (A) incremental value of the CNN misfit beyond
ordinary single-fit loss, AUM, EL2N, and prior errors; (B) CNN-based clinical-stage
results including adjusted false-negative differences. This file is committed before
any training run; all endpoints below are frozen.

## Fits

Identical to the committed backbone harness (`run_oasis3_cnn_backbone.py`): leakage-free
OASIS-3 cohort (N=1360, one earliest T1w session per participant), twofold-downsampled
smwc1 volumes (60x72x60), CNN3D (4 Conv-GroupNorm-ReLU-MaxPool blocks, 8-64 ch, GAP
head), Adam lr 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, batch 16, 50 epochs, leave-one-scanner-out,
seeds 42-44. Nominal budget 6 scanners x 3 seeds = 18 fits; the harness's existing
guard (held-out n < 5) skips the Avanto fold (n=2), so 15 fits execute, as in the
committed backbone run. Avanto's 2 subjects remain in training folds but are never
tested; target analyses cover the 1,358 tested participants. No hyperparameter is
changed; the only additions are recordings (per-epoch margins, EL2N at epoch 20,
per-subject arrays), which consume no training randomness: the training RNG stream
(torch.manual_seed(seed); np.RandomState(seed*100+held) permutations) is untouched,
so fits reproduce the committed backbone run exactly.

## Scores (all per subject; 3-seed means unless stated)

- **cnn_misfit** (primary): cross-fit mean training CE at epoch 50 (the checkpoint the
  paper quotes for the backbone test), averaged over folds where the subject is in
  training, then over seeds. Sensitivity: epochs 10 and 25.
- **ordinary_singlefit**: training CE at epoch 50 from one designated fit per seed: the
  first fold in scanner-size order in which the subject is in training (fold 0 for
  non-TrioTim subjects; fold 1 for TrioTim subjects). A single-fit practitioner score,
  no extra fits, deterministic rule.
- **aum**: negative mean margin (true-logit minus best-other-logit) over epochs 1-50,
  designated fit, higher = harder.
- **el2n**: ||softmax - onehot||_2 at epoch 20, designated fit.
- **prior_errors**: mean held-out error over the five canonical collect() evaluations
  (seeds 42-46, PCA-MLP/IOP harness; `oasis3_cdr_participant_arrays.npz`
  `err_per_seed`). A genuinely prior record from a different architecture.
- Secondary (construction decomposition): cross-fit-averaged variants of
  ordinary/aum/el2n, to separate cross-refit averaging from score identity.

## Target

Per-subject CNN held-out error frequency over the 3 seeds (each tested participant is
evaluated once per seed, when their scanner is held out).

## Endpoint A: incremental value

1. Marginal Spearman rho and failure-detection AUROC of every score vs the target.
2. Rank-partial Spearman of cnn_misfit given each comparator singly, and given all four
   jointly; 95% subject-bootstrap CIs (B=2000), mirroring the S15 methodology.
3. Symmetric check: rank-partial of ordinary_singlefit given cnn_misfit.
4. Zero-prior-error subgroup (prior_errors == 0): marginal rho and AUROC of cnn_misfit.
**Decision rule (frozen)**: "incremental value beyond the comparator set" is claimed
only if the joint rank-partial CI excludes zero; otherwise the result is reported as a
null, exactly as the S15 endpoint was.

## Endpoint B: clinical stage

1. cnn_misfit vs CDR stage (0 / 0.5 / >=1): Spearman rho, p, per-seed rhos, stage
   medians of the misfit percentile (inverted-U replication test against the PCA-MLP
   profile 41.2 / 77.9 / 58.2).
2. False negatives among impaired participants (label 1, tested): FN count out of 3
   seed-trials per subject; binomial GLM with logit link,
   FN/3 ~ C(stage, ref = CDR>=1) + age (z-scored) + sex(male=1); report adjusted odds
   ratios with 95% CIs for CDR 0.5 vs CDR>=1, adjusted predicted FN probability per
   stage, and unadjusted per-stage FN rates alongside.
3. FN-capture: fraction of seed-level false negatives inside the top-20% of
   cnn_misfit (continuity with the published capture-yield panel).
CDR inputs from the bound participant records
(`oasis3_cdr_participant_records.csv`, candidate-audited 2026-10-05).

## Reporting

All endpoints reported regardless of direction; honest nulls stated as nulls. Caveats
carried: CDR also defines the label (label-conditional profile); retrospective audit;
descriptive field; 15 of 18 nominal fits per the small-fold guard. Outputs:
`results/oasis3/oasis3_cnn18_arrays.npz` (every per-subject per-seed quantity) and
`results/oasis3/oasis3_cnn18_study.json` (endpoints). GPU 2.
