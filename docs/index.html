How to read the maps

Movement through space,
not through time.

Each chapter sweeps through axial, coronal and sagittal slices of the same aggregate association map shown in the manuscript. The animation depicts spatial navigation—not disease progression, longitudinal change or training dynamics.

Colors show coefficients standardized within each map, with fixed display limits of −2.6 to +2.6 and an overlay at |display z| > 0.8. This is a display threshold, not a significance threshold. Warm colors indicate relatively higher coefficients, which may still be negative in raw units; colors are not comparable cross-cohort effect sizes.

Orientation, sampling and provenance

L/R denote left/right and P/A posterior/anterior. Coordinates are in millimeters. The three planes traverse their own analyzed-mask coordinate ranges; they do not track a common anatomical landmark across cohorts.

OASIS-3 includes 1,360 participants. Corrected participant-purged ADNI includes 731 acquisitions from 547 participants. OASIS-1 includes 226 participants and uses Schaefer-100 parcel coefficients. OASIS-3 and ADNI display values are standardized over analyzed voxels; OASIS-1 values are standardized over parcels before projection.

The anatomical background is the standard MNI152 template, not an individual participant. The viewer uses an unchanged copy of the original MP4; no participant-level images or identifiers are included. It adds playback controls, not new statistical analyses. Source hashes and display settings are recorded in the provenance record.
